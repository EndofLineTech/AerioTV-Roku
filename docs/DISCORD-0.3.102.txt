**AerioTV for Roku — Beta 0.3.102**

**Set up your Roku from your phone**
• Direct encrypted phone setup is now enabled in the normal Beta—no special pairing build needed.
• Open phone setup on your Roku, then use a compatible AerioTV phone app to scan the QR code and send your Dispatcharr playlist and selected profiles.
• Imported profiles keep their own sign-ins, supported avatars and existing numeric/Directions PINs. Fresh imports preserve valid shared profile IDs.
• Default-profile PIN protection and Child restrictions stay in place.
• Profiles Sync is a separate choice. Sending setup from your phone does **not** turn Sync on.

**Also fixed**
• An empty optional verification proof no longer prevents a fresh account check.
• New imports retain the sender's default-profile ID without changing an existing Roku default-profile ID.

**Beta testing notes**
Apple and Android protocol encoders were tested against the actual Roku receiver and live Dispatcharr accounts. Physical phone UI and same-network discovery still need Beta-user testing; these are not claimed as fully verified.

Existing/imported PINs are supported. Creating new PINs or a Device Admin PIN is not included yet. Previously documented Beta limitations still apply.
