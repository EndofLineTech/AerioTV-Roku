AerioTV for Roku

Your TV, your way.

Explore live channels and what’s on next with an interactive guide built for your Roku remote. Bring your own compatible TV source, and make your lineup feel right at home.

Get setup help View testing builds

AerioTV is an app, not a TV service. Channels, subscriptions, and DVR storage are not included.

Made for the living room

Everything in one place.

A clear way to find something to watch, built around the sources you already use. What’s available depends on your setup.

01 / DISCOVER

Interactive guide

See what’s on now and coming up, browse program details, search, and organize your channels with favorites and groups.

02 / WATCH

Live TV and more

Move between live channels and the guide with your Roku remote. Browse movies and shows when your connected source makes them available.

03 / RECORD

Server DVR

With a permitted Dispatcharr account, schedule recordings from the guide and manage or play recordings stored on your own server.

Get started

From your setup to your screen.

1. Bring your source

Have your own compatible TV source and any required account information ready. AerioTV does not supply content.

2. Connect on Roku

Open AerioTV, enter your connection details, and let the app load the channels and guide available to you.

3. Settle in

Pick a channel, explore what’s next, and use the features supported by your source and account.
