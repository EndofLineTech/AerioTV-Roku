[          ] R01 Guide and DVR list
Open the authorized Dispatcharr guide, then DVR. Check that Recording Now,
Scheduled, Recent and Series Rules are readable in one list with clear focus;
Back returns to the previous guide location. Empty sections are not actionable.

[          ] R02 Completed or stopped recording
On an already permitted recording, verify actual picture and audible sound.
Press FF and REW, observe the native timeline and resumed picture/sound;
Back returns to the DVR row without changing another recording.

[          ] R03 Growing recording — only if an approved fixture is available
While a permitted server recording is running, open its Recording Now row and
Play / resume. Check actual picture and audible sound, pause/resume, then FF,
REW and Play at a position behind live. Confirm picture/audio after each seek,
the available-window bound, the return to the DVR row and continued playback
as the recording grows. If the recording ends during viewing, record the actual
reader/return behavior separately. SKIP when no approved active fixture exists;
do not create, stop or delete shared server recordings for this worksheet.

[          ] R04 Restore state
Confirm the normal app and saved account/guide preferences remain in place.
Record any UI, focus, caption or seek defect with exact remote steps and build.
