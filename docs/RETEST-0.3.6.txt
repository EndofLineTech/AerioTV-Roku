A. STARTUP RECOVERY — ihp.32
Start with your existing Always use AAC compatibility setting.
Tune 2101 NCAAF | EMU/WIS if it is still available, then a known-good channel
such as ESPN 408. A changed/unavailable event feed is not proof of a Roku bug.
[     ] A01 Normal startup still produces moving video and synchronized audio.
If an actual startup stall occurs, wait without manually retuning. The app
should retry this local connection once on the native buffering-stall error,
or after approximately 25 seconds without reaching playback.
[     ] A02 One startup-retry notice appears and the stream recovers without input.
[     ] A03 Audio mode remains unchanged after recovery.
If the retry also stalls, wait for the final error. The retry budget is one
per explicit tune; it is not a continuous reconnect loop.
[     ] A04 A second failure ends with an error instead of repeated retries.
[     ] A05 A buffering-stall error says buffering stalled, not unsupported codec.
Mark A02-A05 SKIP with a reason if the corresponding failure did not occur.
Do not interrupt a shared server/other viewers just to manufacture a failure.

During startup buffering: open PLAYER OPTIONS -> Stop playback. Wait 30 seconds.
[     ] A06 Stop remains stopped; an old watchdog does not restart video.
During startup buffering: choose another channel through Channels or the guide.
[     ] A07 The previous channel does not return later from a stale retry.
Start a channel -> Back to mini-guide while still buffering, if possible.
[     ] A08 Startup/retry can complete in mini-guide without forcing fullscreen.
After successful playback, pause for at least 30 seconds -> resume.
[     ] A09 The startup watchdog does not retune paused/established playback.
If coordinated with another viewer on that channel:
[     ] A10 Local startup recovery does not stop the other viewer.
