🎉 **AerioTV for Roku v0.3.28 — Testing Preview**

A big update since v0.3.8! Uses your existing **Dispatcharr** server—no extra service or container.

**🎬 Movies & TV Shows**
• Browse, search, filter and sort movies and series.
• Episode browsing, Continue Watching, resume, watchlists and hidden titles.
• Choose and remember available source versions on authorized accounts.

**⏪ Catch-up, Restart & Rewind**
• Replay completed programs or restart a show that's still airing.
• Provider-backed rewind: **up to 60 minutes since tuning** on catch-up channels.
• **1/2/5-minute skips**, hold-to-preview seeking, commit/cancel and pause preservation.
• **Go Live**, historical program information and catch-up icons beside channel numbers.

**📺 Navigation & Reliability**
• **Live TV / VOD / Settings** in the guide header and categorized settings.
• **Hold OK** for app player options; tap OK for info. Fullscreen `*` remains Roku-owned.
• Faster guide startup, bounded caching, improved recovery/Retry and diagnostics.
• Live TS-reader fix and improved episode loading.

**Testing:** 44 automated suites passed; maintainer physical checks **50/50 PASS**. Tested on Streaming Stick 4K with Roku OS 15.3.4 and Dispatcharr 0.31.0.

**Heads-up:** Rewind/restart depend on provider archives—not a local buffer. Some VOD sources and descriptions remain unavailable. This is a sideloaded testing preview, not a Roku Store app.
