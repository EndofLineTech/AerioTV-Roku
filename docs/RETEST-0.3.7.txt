and ESPN 408. An expired/unavailable event feed is not itself a Roku defect.
[     ] B01 Normal startup still gives moving picture and synchronized audio.

media startup. AAC discovery has its own earlier 20-second limit.
[     ] B02 One retry notice appears and playback recovers without input.
[     ] B03 Recovery retains the selected audio setting/output profile.

[     ] B04 Playback stops with an error rather than entering a reconnect loop.
